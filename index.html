Questions

Questions businesses ask us.

Do you replace our existing IT provider?

Not necessarily. Many businesses keep their provider for day-to-day support and use ElyTech for decisions, projects and holding suppliers to account. If a change would genuinely serve you better, we'll help you make it smoothly.

We think we've been hacked. Can you help now?

Call 01353 880082. We'll help you contain the problem first, then work out what happened and how to stop it happening again.

A supplier has quoted us for new kit. Is it fair?

That's exactly what an Ask an IT Manager session is for. We'll review the quote against what you actually need and tell you plainly.

Do you come on-site?

Yes. We work on-site across Ely, Cambridge, East Cambridgeshire, Suffolk and Norfolk, and remotely anywhere in the UK.

How is work priced?

Every engagement gets a fixed, written quote before anything starts. The ongoing Fractional IT Manager service is a simple monthly fee you can cancel any month.

Can you help us get Cyber Essentials?

Yes. We prepare your systems and policies for the scheme and support you through the self-assessment, so certification goes smoothly.
