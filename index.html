Example engagement · Legal

A conveyancing firm in Ely

12 staff · one office

The situation
Two shared logins on reception, no multi-factor sign-in, a former employee's mailbox still active, and a recent email asking a client to send money to new bank details.
What we'd do
An IT Health Check in the first week, then an Identity & Access Lockdown: multi-factor sign-in on every account, leavers' access removed, admin rights reduced, hidden mail-forwarding rules checked, and a short staff briefing on spotting payment fraud.
The result
Every account protected, a written risk report for the partners, and a simple joiner, leaver and bank-detail-change process the team actually follows.
