When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you give us: your name, business name, email address, phone number if you provide it, team size, the service you're interested in and your message.

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you go ahead, to provide and invoice our services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

We don't sell or share your information for marketing. Enquiries are processed by our website host, Netlify, and delivered to our email provider, who handle it on our behalf.

If you don't become a client, we delete enquiry details within 12 months. Client records are kept for as long as UK accounting and legal rules require.
